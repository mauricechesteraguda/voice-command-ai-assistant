# Domain Glossary

- **Edge runtime**: the local device-side voice-assistant runtime that captures, processes, and speaks a conversation.
- **Control plane**: the remotely operated platform that manages desired configuration, deployment, observability, and recovery for edge workloads.
- **Cloud profile**: a named, validated deployment target describing provider, region, cluster, data services, identity, and cost/security posture.
- **Promotion**: the controlled movement of an immutable platform/application version between GitOps environments.
- **Consent**: an explicit, purpose-specific permission from the operator for a data or network action.
- **Telemetry**: operational signals emitted to measure health, performance, and reliability; it excludes conversation content by default.
- **Credential**: a secret or token that authorizes access to a resource; credentials are distinct from configuration and telemetry.
- **Audit event**: an immutable record of a security- or administration-relevant action and its outcome.
- **Recovery point objective (RPO)**: the maximum acceptable age of recoverable data after a failure.
- **Recovery time objective (RTO)**: the maximum acceptable time to restore an agreed service after a failure.
